About

Named after a village near Elmina.

Bando is a village near Elmina, where our founder grew up. The name is a reminder of where practical knowledge is needed most.

Bando Books is an independent publisher registered in Ghana. We publish work that helps people build things that last: businesses, teams, cooperatives and careers. Our first title, From Effort to Systems by Sule Abdul Rahman, comes from his work alongside farmers, cooperatives, agripreneurs and organisations in Northern Ghana.
